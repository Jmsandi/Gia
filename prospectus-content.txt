Our Brief History

Gbonkolenken International Academy (GIA), established in September 2023 by Mr. Issa Koroma—a visionary educator—has developed into a leading educational institution that offers numerous opportunities for students, parents, and community members. Created to provide quality education at an affordable rate, it has become a hub of academic and technological excellence accessible to people of all economic backgrounds. Our dedication to nurturing young minds and fostering a love for learning remains unwavering. Today, we take pride in being a top institution preparing students for success in a constantly evolving world. A sturdy fence encloses the school. It features a three-story building with thirty classrooms, an expanded administrative wing, a learning resource center/library, a science lab, an ICT room, and a kindergarten playground. It benefits from 24/7 electricity, reliable internet, dependable, safe drinking water, and modern sanitation facilities.

OUR CORE VALUES

At GIA, we are committed to cultivating a community that upholds Godliness, respect, accountability, commitment, and excellence. These core values underpin our school’s culture and influence our decisions and actions. We are best encapsulated by the acronym GRACE, which embodies our key principles. 



Godliness

We believe that cultivating Godliness is essential for achieving success in life. Our goal is to provide a comprehensive and engaging education that stresses character development through values such as honesty, love, peace, and compassion for others, regardless of background, race, sexual orientation, or economic status. Encouraging godliness in students can strengthen their faith and deepen their connection with God. Additionally, it promotes lifelong virtues like integrity and honesty.

Respect

We believe that respect is crucial for building and maintaining relationships, as well as for forming partnerships with students, parents, and community members. We teach our learners to value others' opinions, even when they differ. Promoting respect helps students develop strong, positive relationships with peers, teachers, and staff. Furthermore, respect promotes self-awareness by helping students understand how their words and actions impact others.

Accountability

We believe that taking responsibility for one's actions, whether positive or negative, is crucial for meaningful change. Accountability encourages students to own their learning, which can lead to better grades and academic success. When students are responsible for their work, they stay focused, motivated, and actively involved in their studies. Additionally, they learn how to communicate effectively with teachers and their peers.

Commitment

We emphasize training our students to value “commitment” because it offers significant academic, personal, and social advantages. A strong commitment to learning enhances motivation, raises self-confidence, which, combined with good time management skills, helps students prioritize tasks and manage their schedules efficiently. It also fosters teamwork, cultivates leadership abilities, and encourages accountability, as being dedicated to a goal or project means taking responsibility for their actions.





Excellence

We firmly believe that striving for excellence distinguishes us from other educational institutions. By inspiring our young learners to aim for excellence, we foster critical thinking and problem-solving skills. Excellence promotes healthy competition and nurtures leadership qualities. It equips our students to succeed in academics, careers, and personal growth.

Our Future

As we look optimistically to the future, we stay dedicated to our mission and core values, while welcoming new ideas and initiatives that help us remain innovative. We are excited to continue offering an affordable, top-quality education that prepares our students for success in an increasingly complex and connected world.



Academic Programs

The GIA curriculum is officially recognized and approved by the Ministry of Basic and Senior Secondary School Education (MBSSE) in Sierra Leone, West Africa. While GIA operates as a private school, it is mandated to follow the curriculum established by the MBSSE. Its students sit for all the national examinations, which include NPSSE, BECE, and WASSCE, conducted by the West African Examinations Council (WAEC).

GIA offers the following programs: 

1. Day Care School

 2. Nursery (Kindergarten) School

 3. Primary (Elementary) School 

4. Junior Secondary School (JSS) 

5. Senior Secondary School (SSS). 

ADMISSION PROCEDURES – Admission to the following:

1. Day Care School – Parents/Guardians are required to provide a Birth Certificate and a Medical Certificate/Document from a reputable doctor.

2. Nursery/Kindergarten School - Provide a Birth Certificate and Medical Certificate/Document from a reputable doctor. (A transfer certificate is required if the student attends a previous school)

3. Primary School - Provide a Birth Certificate, a transfer certificate, and a previous School Report Card, where applicable.

4. Junior Secondary School - Provide a Birth Certificate, transfer certificate, and previous School Report Card, where applicable.

5. Senior Secondary School - Provide a Birth Certificate, transfer certificate, and previous School and BECE Report Cards, where applicable.

For new admissions at all levels (Primary to Senior Secondary School), pupils are required to take entrance exams and/or participate in interviews. NO EXCEPTIONS.

Withdrawal Policy

Parents MUST provide written notice of withdrawal, and fees may apply depending on the timing of the withdrawal. No tuition fee is refundable if the student has received the school supplies and attended for one month or more.

 A rental fee of NL 400 will be charged for the graduation gown.

 Parents/students will be charged for any damage or loss to the gown.



Books:

 A rental fee of Le 300 per book will be charged for the academic year.

 Books are rented for the entire academic year, and parents or students are responsible for returning them by the end of the year.

 Parents or students will be billed for any damage to or loss of the books.

Penalties for Overdue:

 Parents and students will be charged a late fee of Le 50 per day for any rented item not returned on time.

School Policies

GIA has several policies to guide the behavior of our students, teachers, parents, and other staff members. Below are a few of these policies:

 Attendance Policy: Regular attendance is mandatory, and parents must notify the school via phone or letter of any absence. Three (3) unexcused absences will result in student suspension until an explanation is provided.

Behavioral Policy: All students are expected to behave respectfully and responsibly. Disciplinary measures may be enforced if these expectations are not met.

Bullying Policy: We enforce a strict zero-tolerance policy on bullying. Any incidents will be reported, investigated, and handled promptly

Academic Integrity Policy: We expect each student to uphold the highest standards of academic integrity, and plagiarism or cheating will not be tolerated.

Communication Policy: We value communication with parents and encourage regular updates. Parents can expect to receive regular reports on their child’s progress. 

Uniform Policy: Our uniform requirements are clearly outlined and strictly enforced to ensure consistency and fairness. We designed our uniform to be comfortable, durable, and well-fitting, ensuring that students can focus on their studies without distraction from fashionable attire.
